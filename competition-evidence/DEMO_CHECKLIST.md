# One-minute ClearStep demo

1. Open `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Point out the headline: “Confusing homework? Let’s make it clear.”
3. Click the Math example.
4. Click Explain directions.
5. Show “Thinking…” and then the structured summary, steps, and requirements.
6. Click Start Focus Mode.
7. Mark the first step complete and move to the next step.
8. Return to the full explanation and mention that the student still does the work.

Use the real AI response. Do not present the illustrative Before/After panel as an AI result.
