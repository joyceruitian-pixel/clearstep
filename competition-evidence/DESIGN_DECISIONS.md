# ClearStep design decisions

- **Clarity first:** The headline, input, and Explain directions action appear before supporting information.
- **Confusion to clarity:** The Before/After panel, numbered steps, progress bar, and structured result sections repeat the product idea without adding clutter.
- **Middle-school friendly:** Language is short and direct, with calm teal and cream colors instead of childish decoration.
- **Teacher-first:** The interface repeatedly says that teacher directions come first and that ClearStep does not do the assignment.
- **Local by default:** Explanations and Focus Mode progress stay in the current browser for now; a real database is deferred.
- **Accessible motion:** Focus styles and reduced-motion support are included, and loading/results use live announcements.
