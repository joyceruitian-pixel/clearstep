# ClearStep user-testing guide

Do not fill in results until real middle-school testers try the app.

## Five tasks

1. Open ClearStep and explain what you think it does.
2. Paste a confusing assignment into the box.
3. Generate an explanation and find the most important requirements.
4. Use Focus Mode to move through the steps and mark one complete.
5. Refresh the page and check what information remains on this computer.

## Five feedback questions

- Was ClearStep easy to understand?
- Did the explanation make sense?
- Were the steps helpful?
- Was anything confusing?
- What would you improve?
