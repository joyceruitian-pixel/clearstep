2:50–3:15 · Responsible AI

Designed for safe learning.

01

Explain, don’t complete

ClearStep breaks down directions but refuses to do the student’s homework.

02

Protect information

The website warns students not to share names, addresses, school details, or passwords.

03

Teacher first

Students are reminded to compare the AI explanation with the teacher’s original directions.

It also refuses harmful or unrelated requests, uses friendly language, and avoids inventing requirements.
