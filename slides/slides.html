04 · The technology

How ClearStep uses AI

The student’s directions stay at the center of the process.

Teacher’s directionsThe student pastes only the assignment instructions they want explained.
→
Secure backend + OpenAIThe server sends the request with ClearStep’s safety instructions. The API key stays server-side.
→
Clear explanationThe AI returns structured summary, steps, requirements, and safe teacher reminders.

Model used by the project: gpt-4o-mini. ClearStep does not train its own model.

06 · Responsibility

Designed for safe learning.

→

Directions, not answers

ClearStep explains the assignment and refuses requests to complete it for the student.

✦

Privacy first

The interface warns students not to enter names, school details, passwords, or other private information.

▣

Teacher first

Students are reminded to compare the AI explanation with the teacher’s original directions.

The safety plan also covers harmful or off-topic requests, age-appropriate language, and no invented requirements.
