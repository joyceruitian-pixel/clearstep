# ClearStep Recording Demo Checklist

## Before recording

- Start the local server with the command in `NOTES.md`.
- Open `http://localhost:3000/` and `http://localhost:4173/slides/slides.html`.
- Close unrelated tabs and hide private browser notifications.
- Test the microphone and make sure the recording is horizontal.
- Confirm the browser shows no API key, full name, address, school details, or other private information.
- Have this exact assignment ready to paste:

  > Read pages 12–18 of your textbook. Write a one-paragraph response explaining the main character’s motivation. Include two pieces of evidence from the story and submit your response by Friday.

- Keep the presenter notes nearby, but speak naturally.

## 80-second live demonstration plan

1. **0:00–0:15:** Switch from slide 4 to the ClearStep website, paste the prepared assignment, and click **Explain Directions**.
2. **0:15–0:35:** Point to the one-sentence meaning and numbered steps. Describe the actual response, not an imagined one.
3. **0:35–0:48:** Check one step to show the separate progress tracker.
4. **0:48–1:02:** Tap an underlined word such as **motivation** to show its simple definition. Open and close the card once.
5. **1:02–1:12:** Point to **Make it your level** and explain that the selected level changes the next AI explanation.
6. **1:12–1:20:** Scroll to **What to turn in** if it appears, then return to the slideshow.

Prioritize the main AI explanation, steps, vocabulary, and safety. Skip optional features if the response is slow.

## Backup plan

If the live AI request fails, do not fabricate a response or call a hardcoded response live. Stop the recording and use a real successful AI response saved earlier. If clips are edited together, say that the response was recorded earlier.

## After recording

- Return to the slideshow for the AI, testing, safety, learning, and closing slides.
- Keep the final video under four minutes, under 300 MB, and in a horizontal format.
- Review the whole video once with sound on and once with sound off for readability.
- Make sure the teacher’s directions, private information, API key, and unrelated browser content are not exposed.
- Save a copy before uploading or sharing it, then check that the uploaded/shared copy opens.
