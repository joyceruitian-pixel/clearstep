# ClearStep Video Presenter Notes

Target length: about 3 minutes 55 seconds. These are speaking prompts, not a script. Use natural eighth-grade wording and personalize them before recording.

| Time | Slide | Speaking points | Advance / switch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | 1. ClearStep | “Hi, this is ClearStep. I built it to make confusing homework directions easier to understand, so students can start with more confidence.” | Advance when the title is introduced. |
| 0:20–0:40 | 2. The problem | “Sometimes I understand the subject but not what the directions want. Students may not always have someone nearby to explain them, and that confusion can make it hard to begin.” | Advance after explaining the personal connection. |
| 0:40–0:52 | 3. The solution | “ClearStep changes complicated directions into a short explanation and clear steps. It explains the work without doing the work.” | Advance after the before-and-after comparison. |
| 0:52–1:00 | 4. Live demo | “Now I’ll switch to the actual website and show how it works.” | Switch manually to the live ClearStep tab. |
| 1:00–1:55 | Live website | Paste the prepared assignment. Explain the real AI result, point to the numbered steps, check one step, tap a vocabulary word, and briefly show the grade selector. | Keep the demo moving. Skip extra features if the AI response takes time. |
| 1:55–2:00 | Live website | If time allows, show “What to turn in” and open “Copy to Notes.” Say that the preview can be pasted into notes. | Return to the slideshow. |
| 2:00–2:25 | 5. How AI works | “The student’s directions go to my secure backend. The backend sends them to OpenAI with ClearStep’s instructions, and the structured result comes back as a summary and steps. The API key is not in the browser.” | Advance after the flow is clear. |
| 2:25–2:50 | 6. Testing | “I tested different assignments, safety requests, saved progress, and Focus Mode. The final MVP Goal 4 rerun had 15 out of 15 fully right cases, and the Focus Mode tests all passed.” | Mention that untested cases are labeled honestly. |
| 2:50–3:15 | 7. Responsible AI | “ClearStep explains directions instead of completing homework, warns students not to share private information, and reminds them that the teacher’s directions come first. It also refuses harmful or off-topic requests.” | Advance after the three cards. |
| 3:15–3:40 | 8. Learning and next | “I learned that building an AI project is not only about making features. Testing, safety, and accessibility are just as important. Next I want more student feedback and more accessibility testing.” | Advance when the future plan is stated. |
| 3:40–3:55 | 9. Thank you | “ClearStep is about helping students understand the task, not just finish it. Thank you.” | End recording on the final slide. |

Keep the total below four minutes. If a live request is slow, do not pretend it is live: use a separately recorded real successful response and explain that it was recorded earlier.
